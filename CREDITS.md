## Maps and diagrams

- **Live satellite tab:** Esri World Imagery basemap tiles (`server.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Imagery/MapServer`), © Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics and the GIS User Community. Fallback tiles: © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) (ODbL).
- **PDAU tab image:** `assets/plan-step2035.jpg` — extract of the City of Vienna *Stadtentwicklungsplan (STEP 2035)* (Wien-Plan), © Stadt Wien / MA 18, open data (Stadt Wien Datenkatalog).
- **POS tab image:** `assets/plan-zoning-pos.jpg` + `assets/legend-zoning.png` — *Flächenwidmungs- und Bebauungsplan* extract served by the Vienna GeoServer WMS (`data.wien.gv.at/daten/wms`, layer `GENFLWIDMUNGOGD`), © Stadt Wien / MA 21, open data.
- Mapping library: [Leaflet](https://leafletjs.com/) 1.9.4, BSD-2-Clause licence.
- **QR code:** generated at runtime by [api.qrserver.com](https://api.qrserver.com/) (goqr.me) from the page URL; no key or dependency required.

## Video

`vienna-overview.mp4` is the project’s local overview film, a web-optimised 1080p cut of *Scenic View of Iconic Vienna Skyline and Architecture* by **Eleonora Vokueva**, used under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/). The source clip is available on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/video/scenic-view-of-iconic-vienna-skyline-and-architecture-37677778/); the same source URL is provided as a remote fallback stream in the video modal. `assets/video-poster.jpg` is a still frame from that clip. `vienna-overview-original.mp4` is an archival source file and is not referenced by the site.
